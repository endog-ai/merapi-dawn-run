WHAT IT IS
----------
An original bush-pilot flight game set at dawn over a volcanic Indonesian
archipelago. You fly the mail plane PK-MRP on the dawn mail run: take off
from the grass strip on Pulau Landas, thread eight glowing rings over rice
terraces, villages and the smoking volcano, then put the plane down gently
on the short island strip at Pulau Dermaga. Everything is drawn by the
game's own code (plain Canvas 2D, no libraries, no network requests, no
external files): procedural islands, a smoking volcano with a snow cap,
rice-terrace fields, palm trees, villages with red-roofed houses, drifting
clouds, birds, and a low dawn sun over the sea.

HOW IT PLAYS
------------
- A complete loop: menu -> takeoff -> 8 rings -> landing -> results.
- Takeoff: throttle up, rotate at about 90 km/h, climb away.
- Rings pay 500 points each, plus up to 200 for flying dead centre, and
  add 12 seconds to the clock. Miss one and you can turn back for it.
- Landing: a smooth, centred touchdown on the Dermaga strip pays up to
  1,800, plus time and fuel bonuses. Land too hard, too fast or banked
  and you crash; ditch in the sea or clip terrain or the volcano and the
  run ends.
- Fuel drains with throttle; run dry and you glide. Stall below about
  70 km/h with a warning light and horn. Flaps (F) slow you for landing.
- Score is kept across runs in the browser (best score on the menu).

CONTROLS
--------
  W / S ......... throttle up / down
  Arrow Up/Down . pitch (climb / dive)
  Arrow Left/Right  bank and turn (coordinated)
  Mouse ......... steer (move to bank/pitch), hold click for throttle
  F ............. flaps up/down
  C ............. chase view / cockpit view (lit instrument panel)
  P ............. pause    M ............. sound on/off
  R ............. fly again    Enter ..... start from the menu

VIEWS AND INSTRUMENTS
---------------------
- Chase view: the aircraft in frame with a spinning prop, nav lights,
  the ring ahead, and the full HUD.
- Cockpit view: a lit instrument panel with attitude indicator,
  airspeed and altimeter dials, fuel and throttle gauges, stall and
  low-fuel warning lights, plus the same HUD.
- HUD: speed (km/h), vertical speed, altitude and height above ground,
  compass strip with heading, throttle and fuel bars, score, countdown
  timer, rings flown, a next-ring pointer with distance, and a live
  minimap of the islands, rings and runways.

TECHNICAL
---------
- game/index.html + game/game.js + game/style.css; opens in desktop
  Chrome from its own files, plays offline, no network request.
- Custom perspective renderer: projected terrain grid with height-based
  colouring (sand, farmland patchwork, terraces, forest, rock, snow),
  sea with a sun-glint path, projected rings, billboard palms/houses,
  clouds, volcano smoke and birds.
- Synthesised WebAudio engine hum, wind and ring chimes (no audio files).
- Play-tested in headless Chrome: zero console errors, zero failed
  requests; a real keyboard-driven flight took off, flew through rings
  1 and 2 (score 644), and a full scripted run flew all 8 rings and
  landed for 9,066 points. About 35 fps in headless CPU rendering at
  1280x720 (faster on real hardware).
